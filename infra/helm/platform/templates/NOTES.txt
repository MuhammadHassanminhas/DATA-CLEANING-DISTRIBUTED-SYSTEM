Reach the coordinator from a worker on THIS laptop (cert SANs cover
localhost/127.0.0.1). NOTE: port-forward reaches the cluster only from the
machine running it — a worker on a DIFFERENT Internet connection needs the
public ingress from Step 1.5.5, not this.

  # then point a worker at https://localhost:8443

  # then https://localhost:8444

Step 1.5.5, not this chart.
